Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ChatMoji

Is ChatMoji really free?

Yes! ChatMoji is completely free to use. No hidden fees, no premium subscriptions, no credit card required. Just pure emoji-powered fun!

How does the emoji conversion work?

ChatMoji automatically analyzes every word you type and matches it with our database of 500+ emoji mappings. For words we don't have mapped, we use smart fallback algorithms to assign appropriate emojis based on word characteristics.

Can I turn off the emoji conversion?

The emoji conversion is what makes ChatMoji special! However, we understand some users might want options, so this feature is on our roadmap for future updates.

Does ChatMoji work on mobile devices?

Absolutely! ChatMoji is fully responsive and works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts beautifully to any screen size.

Is my data safe with ChatMoji?

Your privacy and security are our top priorities. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. We never sell your information to third parties.
